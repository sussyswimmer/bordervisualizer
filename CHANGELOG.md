# Changelog

All notable changes to Rimlight are listed here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Edge glow.** A soft two-color glow along the edges of every monitor. It's click-through, stays on top, never takes focus, and doesn't show up in Alt+Tab or on the taskbar. It stays sharp on mixed-DPI setups, rebuilds itself when you plug in, remove or rearrange a monitor, and comes back on its own after a graphics driver reset. ([#8](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/8))
- **Listens to what your PC plays.** Rimlight hears system audio through WASAPI loopback and never opens the microphone. When you switch between speakers and headphones, it follows the new output device. ([#9](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/9))
- **Beat detection.** Rimlight tracks loudness and bass and detects kicks, with a tempo estimate. It adapts to your volume, so the glow moves the same whether the music is loud or quiet, and it notices when the music stops. ([#2](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/2), [#5](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/5), [#7](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/7), [#27](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/27))
- **Music Sync and Idle Glow.** In Music Sync, brightness follows the loudness, every beat sends a pulse through the glow, and the colors drift slowly around the screen. Idle Glow breathes gently on a 6-second cycle. When the music stops, the glow fades to Idle Glow (or hides, if you choose) after 2 seconds, and it's back within 150 ms when the music returns. ([#11](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/11))
- **Colors from album art.** Rimlight picks two vivid colors from the cover of whatever is playing in an app that shows up in Windows' media controls, and tunes them so they glow well. When the track changes, the glow crossfades to the new colors over 800 ms. It recognizes covers with no real artwork and uses your own colors for those, and a dark cover with one bright logo still gets two glowing colors. ([#12](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/12), [#18](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/18), [#20](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/20), [#27](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/27), [#29](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/29))
- **Easy on your PC.** The glow draws nothing while the picture doesn't change and only 10 frames a second while it breathes in Idle Glow. On battery, it's capped at 30 frames a second at half resolution by default. ([#14](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/14))
- **Pauses when nobody can see it.** The glow stops drawing and listening while the screen is locked, the display is off, the PC sleeps or you switch to another user. A game, video or presentation that fills a screen pauses the glow on that screen only, and a screen saver or Windows' presentation mode pauses it everywhere (Pause in fullscreen apps turns both off). When you unplug the charger, your On battery choice applies: keep going, save power, or pause. ([#19](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/19))
- **Tray icon.** Rimlight lives in the notification area as a glowing rounded square, dimmed while the glow is off. Click it to turn the glow on or off, double-click it to open Settings, and point at it to see what's playing. Its menu turns the glow on or off, changes the mode, switches between album and manual colors, applies a preset, opens Settings, checks for updates, opens the logs folder, or quits. ([#1](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/1), [#14](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/14), [#15](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/15), [#18](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/18), [#22](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/22))
- **Shortcut, one copy, starts with Windows.** Press Ctrl+Alt+L anywhere to turn the glow on or off, or record another shortcut in Settings, which warns you when another app already uses it. Starting Rimlight again opens Settings in the copy that's already running. It starts quietly in the tray when you sign in, which you can turn off. The very first start opens Settings with a welcome banner: "Play some music, then look at the edges of your screen." ([#22](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/22), [#29](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/29))
- **Settings window.** A Fluent window that follows Windows' light or dark mode, with six pages. Appearance has a live preview, the shape sliders and five presets: Aurora, Sunset, Neon, Ember and Minimal. Color switches between album art and your own two colors, and shows what's playing with the colors taken from it. Motion sets the mode, what happens when it's quiet and the sensitivity, with a sound level meter. Displays picks the monitors that glow, by name, and a frame rate limit. Behavior covers fullscreen apps, battery, hiding the glow from screen capture, starting with Windows, the shortcut and automatic updates. About has the version and the privacy promise. Every change applies at once, with no Save button, and the window works with the keyboard and screen readers, in high contrast and at 200% text size. ([#16](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/16), [#24](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/24))
- **Automatic updates.** Install Rimlight with `RimlightSetup.exe` (or `RimlightSetup-arm64.exe`) from GitHub Releases. It checks for a new version 30 seconds after it starts and every 12 hours, downloads it in the background, and installs it the next time it starts, or right away with Restart now. With Update automatically turned off, it only checks when you ask. This check is the only thing Rimlight sends over the internet. ([#21](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/21), [#28](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/28))
- **For contributors:**
  - `wav-analyze` runs the beat detector on any WAV file and writes the beats, the tempo, a CSV and a plot. It can also generate synthetic test tracks, and there's an analyzer benchmark. All of it runs on Linux too. ([#13](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/13))
  - Continuous integration builds and tests every pull request on Linux and Windows, labels it from its title, and sorts it into release-note sections. ([#10](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/10))
  - An 8-hour soak test runs the beat detection, lighting and color code the way the app does, for hours of synthetic music in a few minutes. It reports allocations, frame cost and memory, and a 2-minute version runs in CI. The results are in `docs/PERF.md`. ([#25](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/25))
  - `--demo` plays a seamless 24-second loop of beats and album colors without any music, for recordings. `--debug-visualizer` shows what the beat detector hears (spectrum, levels, beats and tempo), with a live slider for every tuning value and Copy params as JSON. ([#26](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/26))
  - `docs/CORE-API.md` describes the engine's API for the app code: which thread calls what, what allocates, and exactly how each part behaves. ([#27](https://github.com/sussyswimmer/bordervisualizer/pull/27))
  - A contributing guide, security policy, code of conduct, issue forms and a pull request template.
